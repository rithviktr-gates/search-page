About
Product lead driving GTM strategy across the Southeast Asia partner channel. I work with distributors, resellers and ISVs to turn capability into outcomes customers actually buy.
Fourteen years in the regional channel, most of it spent between distributor floors and vendor roadmap reviews. Before Connect I ran partner programmes across Indonesia, Malaysia and Vietnam, where the work was less about pricing sheets and more about helping teams sell an outcome they could actually deliver.
Currently focused on enablement that survives contact with a live quarter: shorter certification loops, shared account plans, and renewal conversations that start twelve months out. Based in Singapore, on the road most of the summit season.
